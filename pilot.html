The advertising engine to promote products to the people who will buy them.

Why the Analytics is worth having

Live location to optimize your shop

Those analytics show where shoppers pause and wait, which aisles they skip, where the biggest bottlenecks are, and which spots work best for visibility actions. We also run cross-correlation across all products to identify which items are bought together, so the shop can turn those patterns into promotions, bundle offers, and merchandising actions.

The pilot with encoding by us includes 3 years of these analytics for free.

FAQ

Common questions

What do I get from a pilot?

Your shop and its products on StartToFind, so customers can find them online and navigate straight to them in your shop. A pilot is also your chance to help shape a platform built to bring more customers to your shop, an investment in your shop's digital independence. Want a specific feature built? This is the moment to partner with us on it.

What does the pilot cost?

The free pilot costs nothing: you encode your products yourself in the app and we help you get started. If you'd rather have us come to your shop and encode your products, the pilot fee is €1 per product, so 400 products cost €400. We agree the product count with you before anything is charged.

How do the 200% ad credit and 3 years of free analytics work?

They come with the pilot with encoding by us. Your pilot fee comes back twice as an ad credit balance to spend on ads in StartToFind: a €400 fee for 400 products becomes €800 in ad credit. You also receive 3 years of free analytics. The ad credit is redeemable once StartToFind publicly launches in your region.

Do I need any technical skills or a website?

No. In the free pilot you encode your products in the app, and we help you with onboarding. If you choose encoding by us, we come to your shop and do it for you.

Is basic participation on StartToFind ever paid?

No. Core listing, search presence, and a digital storefront stay free forever. The only paid part of the pilot is the optional encoding service, and that fee comes back to you as 200% ad credit.

What kind of shops are you looking for first?

Shops that sell physical products are the first focus: bakeries, bookstores, boutiques, specialty grocers, and similar. That's where product-level search and in-store navigation deliver the most value first.
