How onboarding works

We come to you first, then it scales

The pilot rollout is deliberately hands-on at the start, so we can prove the process before automating it.

Step 1 · First pilots

In person

The founding team visits your store, walks it with you, and maps it together on-site. No setup work on your end.

Step 2 · As the cohort grows

Video-guided

A video-guided self-mapping process with live remote support, once the workflow is proven and repeatable.

Step 3 · At scale

Fully automated

A self-service upload-and-map flow that lowers the cost of each additional pilot as the cohort grows.

FAQ

Common questions

What do I get from a paid pilot?

More than a return on your advertising budget. A paid pilot is your chance to help shape and support a platform built to bring more customers to your shop, an investment in your shop's digital independence. Want a specific feature built? This is the moment to partner with us on it.

What does the pilot actually cost?

An indicative one-time fee of €500–€5,000 depending on your site size and how much customization you want (custom filters, catalogue integration, etc.). We agree the exact number with you before anything is charged.

How does the 150% ad credit bonus work?

Your pilot fee is converted into an advertising credit balance on Find-It! at 150% of what you paid. A €1,000 pilot fee becomes €1,500 in future advertising credit. It's redeemable once Find-It! publicly launches in your region.

Do I need any technical skills or a website?

No. For the first pilot cohort, the founding team comes to your store in person and handles the mapping and setup directly.

Is basic participation on Find-It! ever paid?

No. Core listing, search presence, and a digital storefront stay free forever. The paid pilot is an optional, limited, early-stage program to help fund and shape development, not a fee for basic access.

What kind of shops are you looking for first?

Shops that sell physical products are the first focus: bakeries, bookstores, boutiques, specialty grocers, and similar. That's where product-level search and in-store navigation deliver the most value first.
