Inside the app

What's already built, not a roadmap: the working app.

Everything on this page is functioning in the current build today. This isn't a vision deck: it's a walkthrough of the actual product, feature by feature, with real screen recordings and screenshots.

Live in the app

Local Product & Place Search Engine

The core entry point of Find-It!. Customers search for products, shops, services, rooms, or categories and get real, mapped results back, with live counts, sponsored placements from local shops, and friend activity, not a generic web listing.

  • Search across every participating local shop at once
  • Sponsored placements, the advertising layer that funds the free core
  • Discovery of small or unique shops that would otherwise be buried behind big platforms
Live in the app

Browse Every Shop, Building & Campus

Beyond product search, customers can browse the directory itself, filtered by what actually matters to them, not just price.

  • Filter by local origin, accessibility, vegan, or cheapest-first
  • Browse by building, campus, or individual shop
  • Distance and price shown up front on every card
Live in the app

Indoor Mapping & Pathfinding

The map layer that ties everything together, from city-wide search down to campus- and building-level detail. Wheelchair-, stroller-, and delivery-friendly routing is part of the same engine.

  • Built on OpenStreetMap, with every mapped site pinned automatically
  • Route guidance to rooms, shelves, departments, entrances, and exits
  • Community and business-verified corrections keep maps accurate over time
Live in the app

Automated Graph Extraction

Deterministic, lightweight algorithms convert an uploaded floor plan into a pathfinding-ready graph: rooms, aisles, doors, and connections, editable and correctable in the browser. No expensive sensors, no heavy AI training required for the first version.

  • Low cost per site, which is what makes free onboarding possible at all
  • Fast onboarding, with the long-term goal of map creation in minutes
Live in the app

Shopping Lists, Shop Mode & Self-Checkout

Build a list before visiting, then get it turned into a live in-store route with a synced checklist, nearby personalized offers, and budget tracking, down to a working scan-and-pay checkout.

  • Full FindIt Route with live position and a synced, tickable list
  • Location-aware, personalized "nearby advertisements" while you shop
  • FindIt Checkout with wallet or card, currently a working prototype, ready to connect to a real payment provider
Live in the app

Free SME Digital Storefronts

Every participating business gets a step-by-step setup dashboard: building & layout, products & deals, scan & organise, shop info. A lightweight alternative to expensive e-commerce infrastructure.

  • Guided setup checklist, no technical skills required
  • Manage every building, floor plan, and product catalogue from one place
  • This is exactly what the founding team walks pilot partners through, in person

Predictive Advertising & Buyer Interest

This is the engine behind the pilot's 150% ad-credit bonus: an explainable model that scores buying likelihood from real signals, without ever naming individual shoppers, only aggregated counts a shop can size and target advertising against.

  • Aggregated-only, privacy-respecting predictive scoring
  • Surfaces exactly where advertising could grow revenue, product by product
  • Target by likelihood to buy, proximity, similar-product interest, or cross-shop purchase history
Live in the app

Site Analytics & Accessibility Compliance

Every mapped site doubles as an accessibility audit, flagging rooms unreachable by less mobile people, and scoring the building's overall connectivity and graph integrity.

  • Connectivity map with accessible vs. inaccessible edges highlighted
  • Reachability status per room, useful for compliance reporting
  • Network efficiency metrics: average connectivity, floor transit load, graph integrity
Live in the app

Works Everywhere

Same app, same data, responsive down to a phone screen, because most local "before I leave home" searches happen on mobile.

  • Responsive layout, no separate mobile app required to try it
  • Bottom tab navigation on small screens
  • Built on the same live data as the desktop view, no separate mobile dataset
