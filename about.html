Why we exist

A trusted search and navigation layer for physical commerce.

Find-It! is not an indoor mapping vendor. It's a local commerce infrastructure platform — built so physical shops can be as searchable, comparable, and navigable as online stores.

Mission

Make every physical space digitally accessible, searchable, comparable, and navigable

— while giving SMEs the tools to compete with large platforms and brands.

Vision

Choice on merit, not just convenience

A world where customers don't choose large retailers only because they're more visible or familiar — where every business can offer a trusted, predictable, digitally supported shopping experience.

The story

Why this got built

It started with a founder's own frustration: arriving in an unfamiliar place — a new city, a new campus, a new building — and not being able to figure out where anything actually was. Not the big-picture "which city block," but the small, everyday failure: which shop sells this, which floor is that on, is it even worth walking there. That gap between "technically searchable" and "actually findable" turned into a deeper question.

"If indoor navigation, local product search, and digital shop visibility are technically possible, why are they not widespread?"

The answer, it turned out, wasn't technology. Every piece of what Find-It! does — floor-plan parsing, pathfinding, product search, propensity modeling — is buildable today. What's missing is adoption: existing solutions are too expensive, too fragmented, or built for enterprise clients with procurement teams, not the independent bakery or bookstore down the street. So Find-It! starts from the opposite end — free at the core, hands-on for the first pilots, and built by combining an interest in algorithms, accessibility, retail behavior, and digital market fairness into one platform.

Right now, that means it's being built solo, in public, one pilot conversation at a time — with the rest of the team (developers, growth, accessibility advisors, an eventual commercial lead) being assembled as the Vienna pilot proves the model works.

What makes this different

Not an indoor mapping vendor

Find-It! is a scalable local commerce infrastructure platform. The differentiation isn't any single feature — it's combining all of these into one adoption-first system, something no single competitor currently does for independent shops:

Free automated indoor mapping
Local product search
SME digital storefronts
Personalized comparison
In-shop navigation
Accessibility guidance
Hyper-local advertising
Network effects

Investing in Find-It! isn't investing in a mapping tool. It's investing in a new search layer for physical commerce — a platform where physical shops become as searchable, comparable, and navigable as online stores already are.

The opportunity

A growth market, still underserved at the SME level

Indoor positioning, indoor navigation, and location-based services are projected to keep growing strongly, driven by smart buildings, retail analytics, healthcare, and transport. At the same time, local commerce remains underdeveloped for SMEs: consumers increasingly start product searches online, but smaller businesses are rarely visible in those searches. The largest opportunity isn't selling indoor maps — it's becoming the trusted discovery and navigation layer that local commerce currently doesn't have.

Strategy

Density first, then network effects

The main barrier in this market isn't technological feasibility — it's adoption. Find-It! focuses on a zero-barrier model: free onboarding, automated deterministic mapping to keep costs low, and a consumer-facing search engine that creates real demand. We start with local SMEs and public institutions in one city — Vienna — to build enough density that search actually works, before expanding.
