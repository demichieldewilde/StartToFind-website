The problem

Local shops lose out in three ways today

Not because they're worse. The system that decides what customers see wasn't built for them.

1

Search friction, online

Customers search nearby and mostly see big chains and marketplaces. Independent shops are invisible before anyone even walks in.

2

Search friction, in-store

Customers who do walk in still can't find the product, room, or shelf they're after. Wasted time for them, wasted staff time for the shop.

3

Advertising you can't target

Big retailers optimize every ad euro using customer and product data. Independent shops usually don't have that data, so budget gets spent on guesswork.

How StartToFind closes the gap

Free at the core. Built for shops, not just search engines.

Every feature below is already working in the app today. See the full walkthrough on the Product page.

🔎

Always free to be found

A digital storefront, product listings, and search presence discoverable by distance, local origin, sustainability, and price. No website or ad budget required.

🧭

Guided indoor navigation

Floor plans become navigable maps automatically. Customers get routed straight to the shelf, room, or counter they need.

📊

Advertising that gets smarter

Every search and in-store interaction feeds predictive models that make ad spend work harder over time, the same targeting big retailers use.

The unfair advantage

Advertising that actually learns

Every search, every in-store route, every "add to list" is a signal. StartToFind turns that data into an explainable predictive model, scoring how likely a shopper is to buy, so a shop can put advertising budget exactly where it will land.

This is the same data-driven targeting big retailers already have, and it's why the pilot's advertising credit is worth more than its face value: it buys access to a targeting engine, not just banner space.

  • Surfaces exactly where advertising could grow revenue, product by product
  • Target by likelihood to buy, proximity, similar-product interest, or purchase history
